THE LEGEND

Gramado, guardian of dreams

Once there was a man weary of a world grown grey and noisy, searching for a meaning he'd lost along the way. One night, a kingfisher with blue, rust and white feathers slipped into his dream and promised to lead him to a wonderful place.

On waking, Gramado — for that is his name — set off in search of this paradise, crossing deserts, mountains and towering forests. Exhausted, he finally fell asleep at the foot of a tree. The kingfisher reappeared and whispered a simple truth: "What you're looking for, you won't find at the end of the world." The bird led him to the banks of the Chassezac.

There, Gramado spoke with the dwellers of the place — a trout that had finally swum upstream, a stone carried along by the flood just as it had dreamed, a fox who knew every trick of the world, a donkey who had found his meadow, a goat who had stayed close to her family, a hen who never missed a sunrise, an oak that gave shade to everyone. And the kingfisher asked him the question that changed everything: "Do you remember what you wanted to build, when you were small?" Gramado remembered: harmony. A place where everyone would be happy, where nature would be beautiful, where people would smile at one another.

So he began to build, through storms, brigands and red tape. When his work was done, the grey clouds parted, nature grew stronger, birds sang more clearly, flowers opened — and people finally smiled. Since then, no one has seen Gramado again. Some say he went off in search of other rivers; others, that he became as elusive as the kingfisher himself. But when the light falls just right on the water of the Chassezac, a flash of blue sometimes appears — and those who see it remember, for the space of a wingbeat, their childhood dream.

The characters

Gramado

A man who set out in search of lost meaning, and ended up founding this place on the banks of the Chassezac — unknowingly fulfilling his childhood dream: to build a place where everyone would be happy.

The Kingfisher

A brilliantly coloured guide-bird, it reveals to Gramado that what he was looking for was already there. Its blue isn't really there at all: it's a trick of the light — just as dreams are the joyful reflection of our reality.

The spirit of the place

Rediscover your inner child

Awaken forgotten dreams

Small, gentle, unintended triggers that bring them back to the surface.

Take a real pause

A quiet moment of introspection, at nature's own pace.

Wonder

Surprises, beauty and art installations around every corner.
